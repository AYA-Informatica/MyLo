# Code of Conduct

## Our pledge

MyLo is built to serve everyone in Rwanda — regardless of income, education,
language, disability, gender, ethnicity, religion, or legal literacy. We hold
ourselves to the same standard inside the project that we set for the product.

We pledge to make participation a harassment-free experience for everyone.

## Our standards

Things that make this project work:

- Assuming good faith, especially across languages and levels of expertise
- Explaining rather than gatekeeping — "you should already know this" helps no one
- Accepting feedback on your work, and giving it about the work rather than the person
- Remembering that a contributor may be a first-time programmer, a lawyer who has
  never opened a terminal, or someone writing in their third language

Things that are not acceptable:

- Harassment, insults, or personal attacks of any kind
- Sexualised language or imagery, or unwelcome attention
- Publishing someone's private information without permission
- Dismissing people for their level of legal or technical knowledge
- Sustained disruption of discussion

## Legal information carries weight

One norm is specific to what we build: **do not present guesses as legal fact.**
Contributions that state or imply legal conclusions must be traceable to the
Gazette or clearly marked as uncertain. People may act on what MyLo says, so
confident-sounding invention is a conduct issue here, not just a quality one.

## Scope

This applies in all project spaces — issues, pull requests, discussions, and
reviews — and when representing the project in public.

## Enforcement

Report unacceptable behaviour to the maintainers via a private security advisory
or by contacting the repository owner directly. Reports will be reviewed and
investigated promptly and fairly, and the reporter's privacy will be respected.

Maintainers may remove comments, commits, or contributions that violate this
policy, and may temporarily or permanently ban any contributor for behaviour they
judge inappropriate, threatening, or harmful.
